Abstract Objective:To identify the symptom clusters of patients with chronic heart failure( CHF) and construct a symptom network to
explore the core symptoms and bridge symptoms. Methods:A total of 342 CHF patients who visited a tertiary grade A level Chinese
medicine hospital from September 2022 to December 2023 were selected as the research subjects by using a convenience sampling method.The
( )
general information questionnaire and the Chinese version of the Memorial Symptom Assessment Scale⁃Heart Failure MSAS⁃HF were used
to investigate the patients.Exploratory factor analysis was used to extract the symptom clusters.The concurrent symptom network was
constructed using R software,and the centrality index was calculated.Results:Five symptom clusters were extracted by exploratory factor
analysis:the sickness symptom cluster,the sleep⁃digestion symptom cluster,the dyspnea symptom cluster,the mental and psychological symptom
( )
cluster,and the peripheral congestion symptom cluster.The top three symptoms of node strength centrality are sweating rs=1.918 ,waking up
( ) ( )
from sleep at night rs=1.167 ,and difficulty concentrating rs=0.099 .The top three symptoms of bridge strength centrality are waking up
from sleep at night ( rbs=4.418) ,abdominal distension ( rbs=4.183) ,and easy sleepiness ( rbs=4.093) .Conclusions:The core symptom of
CHF patients is sweating,and waking up from sleep at night,abdominal distension and easy sleepiness play a major bridging role between
symptom clusters.Medical staff should develop precise intervention plans based on relevant target symptoms to improve the efficiency of
symptom management.
Keywords chronic heart failure,CHF; symptom cluster; core symptoms; bridge symptoms; symptom network
摘要 目的：识别慢性心力衰竭（CHF）病人的症状群，并构建症状网络以探索其中的核心症状和桥梁症状。方法：采用便利抽样法，选
取2022年9月—2023年12月就诊于某三级甲等中医院的342例CHF病人作为研究对象。采用一般资料调查表、中文版Memorial心
力衰竭症状评估量表对病人进行调查。症状群的提取采用探索性因子分析，采用R软件构建同期症状网络，并计算中心性指标。结果：探
索性因子分析提取出5个症状群，分别是病感症状群、睡眠⁃消化症状群、呼吸困难症状群、精神心理症状群和外周淤血症状群。节点强
度中心性排名前3位的症状是出汗（r s=1.918）、夜间睡眠憋醒（r s=1.167）、很难集中注意力（r s=0.099）。桥梁强度中心性排名前3位的
症状是夜间睡眠憋醒（r bs=4.418）、腹胀（r bs=4.183）、易困（r bs=4.093）。结论：CHF病人的核心症状是出汗，夜间睡眠憋醒、腹胀和易困
在症状群之间起着主要桥梁作用；医护人员应根据相关靶点症状，制订精准干预方案，以提高症状管理效率。
