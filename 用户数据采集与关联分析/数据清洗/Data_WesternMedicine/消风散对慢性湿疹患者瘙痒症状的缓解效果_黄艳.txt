消风散对慢性湿疹患者瘙痒症状的缓解效果
黄艳，周辉，李培学
贵州中医药大学第一附属医院，贵州 贵阳 550001
摘 要：目的 探讨消风散对慢性湿疹患者瘙痒症状的缓解效果。方法 选取2023年8月—2024年7月本院
收治的慢性湿疹患者60例，随机将其分为实验组和对照组，每组30例。对照组采用常规治疗法，实验组在常规治
疗基础上加用消风散进行治疗。观察治疗前后瘙痒症状评分、皮损面积（EASI评分）、生活质量评分（DLQI）及
治疗有效率，进行统计学分析。结果 治疗后，两组瘙痒评分、EASI评分均较治疗前有所改善（P<0.05），且实
验组改善程度优于对照组（P<0.05）。治疗后，两组患者生活质量评分均较治疗前有所改善（P<0.05），且实验
组改善程度优于对照组（P<0.05）。实验组治疗有效率高于对照组（P<0.05）。结论 消风散能够有效缓解慢性
湿疹患者瘙痒症状，减少皮损面积，提高治疗有效率和患者生活质量，具有显著的临床应用价值。
关键词：消风散；慢性湿疹；瘙痒症状；治疗有效率

The Relieving Effect of Xiaofeng Powder on Pruritus in Patients with Chronic Eczema
HUANG Yan, ZHOU Hui, LI Peixue
The First Affiliated Hospital of Guizhou University of Traditional Chinese Medicine, Guiyang, Guizhou 550001
ABSTRACT：Objective To investigate the clinical effect of Xiaofeng Powder on relieving pruritus in
patients with chronic eczema, and compare the difference with conventional treatment, so as to provide
clinical reference. Methods 60 patients with chronic eczema treated from August 2023 to July 2024 were
randomly divided into experimental group and control group, with 30 cases in each group. The control
group received conventional treatment, and the experimental group received Xiaofeng powder on the
basis of conventional treatment. Itch symptom score, skin lesion area (EASI score), quality of life score
(DLQI) and treatment efficiency were observed before and after treatment, and statistical analysis
was performed. Results After treatment, both groups showed improvement in itching score and EASI
score compared to before treatment (P<0.05), and the improvement in the experimental group was
better than that in the control group (P<0.05). After treatment, the quality of life scores of both groups
of patients improved compared to before treatment (P<0.05), and the improvement degree of the
experimental group was better than that of the control group (P<0.05). The effective rate of treatment
in the experimental group was higher than that in the control group (P<0.05). Conclusion Xiaofeng
Powder can effectively relieve pruritus symptoms of chronic eczema patients, reduce skin lesions,
improve treatment efficiency and quality of life, and has significant clinical application value.
KEYWORDS：Xiaofengsan; Chronic eczema; Pruritus symptoms; Therapeutic effectiveness
